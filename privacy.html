戳戳 · 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年9月5日 · 联系方式：zimo_2000@icloud.com

戳戳是本地优先的。不登录的话，你记的东西一个字节都不会离开这台设备——没有账号、没有云端、没有任何人能看到。

登录之后会存什么

登录是为了换设备不丢东西。开了账号之后，下面这些会存到我们的服务器上（Supabase）：

  • 邮箱：用来登录和找回密码。
  • 你记录的内容：印记、备注、日记名字、页面、纸样、贴纸、鸥币流水、头像。这些是你写的东西，我们不看、不分析、不用于任何其他用途，存在那儿只是为了让你的另一台设备能拿到。

数据库上有行级安全策略，每个账号只能读写自己那一行。这不是承诺，是数据库强制的。

使用情况统计

为了知道哪里做得不好，app 会记录一些使用行为并回传：

  • 打开了哪些页面、用了哪些印章、买了什么、鸥币余额
  • 一个匿名设备编号、app 版本、系统版本、机型、语言
  • 登录之后还有一个账号编号（加盐哈希之后才落盘），只用来认出"手机和 iPad 是同一个人"

不包含你写的任何内容：备注文字、日记名字、记录内容一个字都不会进这份统计。也不包含你的邮箱。

这份日志保留 90 天，之后自动删除。

不做的事

  • 不做广告追踪，不跟任何第三方的数据合并去定向广告
  • 不出售、不交换你的数据
  • 除了下面这几个为我们提供服务的供应商，不共享给任何人

供应商：Supabase（账号和同步）、Brevo（发验证邮件）、Cloudflare（接收使用统计）。他们只做我们委托的那件事。

你能做什么

  • 删除账号：app 里「我的 → 账号 → 删除账号」，云端那一份会被彻底删掉。这台手机上的记录会留着。
  • 导出数据：app 里「我的 → 导出数据」，把你在这台设备上的全部记录打包带走。
  • 不登录：随时可以只当本地 app 用，功能不缺。

儿童

戳戳不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集他们的信息。

政策有变

改动会更新这一页顶上的日期。涉及收集范围变化的，会在 app 里说明。

Record! · Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5th, 2026 · Contact: zimo_2000@icloud.com

Record! is local-first. If you don't sign in, nothing you write ever leaves your device — no account, no cloud, no one else can see it.

What we store once you sign in

An account exists so you don't lose anything when you switch devices. With an account, the following is stored on our server (Supabase):

  • Your email address, for signing in and resetting your password.
  • The things you record: stamps, notes, journal titles, pages, paper styles, stickers, coin history, and your avatar. This is your content. We don't read it, analyse it, or use it for anything else — it's stored so your other device can fetch it.

Row-level security is enabled on the database, so each account can only read and write its own row. That's enforced by the database, not just promised.

Usage analytics

To learn what isn't working, the app records some usage and sends it back:

  • Which screens were opened, which stamps were used, what was bought, coin balance
  • An anonymous device identifier, app version, OS version, device model, language
  • Once signed in, an account identifier (salted and hashed before it is stored), used only to recognise that a phone and an iPad are the same person

None of your content is included — not a single word of your notes, journal titles, or entries. Your email address is not included either.

These logs are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically.

What we don't do

  • No advertising tracking; your data is never combined with third-party data for ad targeting
  • We don't sell or trade your data
  • We don't share it with anyone except the service providers below

Providers: Supabase (accounts and sync), Brevo (verification emails), Cloudflare (receiving usage logs). They only do what we ask them to do.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Me → Account → Delete account. The cloud copy is permanently deleted. What's on this phone stays.
  • Export your data: Me → Export data, to take everything on this device with you.
  • Don't sign in: the app works fully as a local app.

Children

Record! is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

Any change updates the date at the top of this page. If what we collect changes, we'll say so in the app.
